# v4.5 CP19 — Concurrency integration hardening

CP19 audits the concurrency work from CP15–CP18 against the wider runtime.

## Contract

- Parser state, callable/module environments, thread/future registries and mutex registries are runtime-local.
- Ordinary values crossing a worker boundary are copied; explicit mutex-backed values are the shared-state mechanism.
- Process-global facilities (cwd/environment/processes) remain process-global by nature and are not silently virtualized.
- Separate Parser/Engine instances may execute concurrently; no language-level worker handle is process-global.
- Existing build/template output remains deterministic when language concurrency is unused.
- FFI and promoted embedding concurrency are certified in CP22/CP27 once those surfaces exist.

## Certification

`tests/v45_concurrency_hardening.sh`, the CP16–CP18 suites, Engine concurrency/reload tests, and TSan are the gates for this checkpoint.
