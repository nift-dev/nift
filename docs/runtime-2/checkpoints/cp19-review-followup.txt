Admission boundary

cp19-refusal-review.json mechanically catalogs all 28 session-specific reason
literals, with source locations and A/B/C classifications. A means mandatory
CP19 gap; B means an actual documented policy/invalid-input boundary; C means
inherited compiler/native debt. A/B or B/C rows require their stated contextual
predicate: a reason name is not blanket permission for all inputs it rejects.
D-054 forbids fallback; it does not waive otherwise required language support.

The executable curated boundary has 25 cases per dispatch. 8 probes are
admitted, 9 expose A gaps, 4 are B policy/invalid-input cases, and 4 expose C debt.
Each case also runs legacy and a recorded CP18 stateless counterfactual.
Across mode1/require this is 50 probes per dispatch: 16 admitted probes and 34
compile refusals; 100 additional setup/state-readback units execute on VM.
Every refusal is before FORBIDDEN and preserves the checked old state. No
refusal is a successful VM execution. These are deliberately selected boundary
cases, not an estimate that only32% of normal Nift is supported.11 of 28 reason
families have direct curated witnesses. Other signature/layout/invariant and
resource-exhaustion guards are source-classified, not executable-certified.

A gaps measured: direct/nested/captured aggregate-kind widening; lambda-alias
rebind; mixed-order named replacement; schema replacement; unrelated named
replacement blocked by a retained loop-location closure; wrapper-induced
false dynamic-shadow; explicit this.v falsely blocked by a global v.

D-038 permits refusing genuinely caller-dependent lookup. It does not permit
classifying those last two false positives as accepted exclusions. Positive
witnesses retain unrelated lexical shadowing, numeric aggregate mutations,
fresh lambda replacement, persistent struct mutation and scalar place calls.

C witnesses distinguish inherited missing bytes/prepared support, legacy native
method precedence (CP18 VM accepts a field-call which legacy rejects), and the
captured-struct receiver defect (CP18 stateless execution reaches status70 on
member access). Their existence does not certify release completeness; they
retain their owning checkpoints' repair/coverage obligations.
